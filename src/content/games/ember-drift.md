A single-seat hauler adrift in the belt. Shoot what you can, dodge what you can't, and
keep the tank off empty.

Blast rocks for score and fuel, but every shot and every second of thrust burns fuel
too — run dry and it's game over even with shields intact. Bigger rocks split into
smaller ones when hit, waves escalate over time, and every third wave brings a boss
that has to be worn down before it leaves. Power-ups drop in: shields, rapid fire,
temporary invincibility, a slow-time field, and fuel refills.

Play solo or drag a second player in — separate controls, a shared field, and shields
that can revive a downed partner. Keyboard (arrows/WASD + fire) or touch-drag both
work. Runs and high scores are saved locally in the browser.
