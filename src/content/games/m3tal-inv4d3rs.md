The classic invader block marches side to side and closer every time it hits the edge,
but this time you're a commando behind sandbags. There's chunky pixel art, big explosions,
and an announcer who shouts the name of every gun you pick up.

**One stage that never ends.** Each wave is faster and fires more, and new enemies keep
joining: rebel soldiers, mini UFOs, crabs, mummies whose breath turns you into a mummy,
riot shields, jetpack divers, and green blobs that split in two.

**Free the prisoners.** POWs drift across the top on balloons. Shoot one loose and he
salutes and drops a crate:
- **H** heavy machine gun, **R** homing rockets, **F** flame shot, **S** shotgun, **L** laser
- **G** five more bombs
- **T** the M3TAL SLUG tank, which takes three hits before you bail out

**Bosses every fifth wave.** H41RBUST3R R1B3RTS carpet-bombs the sand, RUGN4M3 locks a
beam onto you, and B1G SH13E fires homing missiles you can shoot down. They come back
tougher each round. Knock off their guns for bonus points.

**Fight for the board.** Chain kills for a score multiplier up to x5, and clear waves fast
for a speed bonus. You get an extra life every 20,000 points. When you fall, your score goes
straight onto the world top 50 under an auto-made callsign, which you can change.

Phone held sideways: drag the left side to move. Fire is automatic, and BOMB sits on the
right. PC: arrows or A/D, Space to fire, K for a bomb, P to pause.

A free fan tribute, not affiliated with SNK. All art and sound are original.
