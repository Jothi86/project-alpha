Take a photo of a name card, or upload several, and the scanner reads the name,
title, organisation, phone numbers, email and address for you.

**Straight into your contacts:** sign in with the Google account your phone syncs
with, and each card is saved into Google Contacts under a "Name Cards" label. It
shows up on your phone a moment later. If the person is already in your contacts,
the existing entry is updated instead of duplicated. Prefer not to sign in? Each card
downloads as a contact file instead, and one tap saves it.

**From Telegram:** paste your own bot's token on the page, and from then on you just
send card photos to your bot and get the contact file back in the chat.

Handles English and Malay cards, Malaysian numbers in +60 format, two cards in one
photo, and front and back of the same card. Photos are not kept.
