StockScreen ranks every listed US common stock (about 6,000) by a blended
long-term score built from value, quality and dividend measures, including
Piotroski F-Scores.

Beyond the ranking it flags two things plain screens miss: value hidden by
one-off accounting, and catalysts that could start an uptrend.

The data refreshes every night. Add the site to your home screen and it
works like an app, including offline on the last data it downloaded.

Not investment advice. It's a starting list for your own research.
