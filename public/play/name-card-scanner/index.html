Name Card Scanner

Take a photo of a name card. It reads the card and puts the contact on your phone. No typing.

Where your contacts go

Sign in with the Google account your phone syncs contacts with, and each card is saved straight into it.

Saving to your Google Contacts
Each card downloads as a contact file. Tap it to save.

One card per photo works best. Two cards side by side, or the front and back of one card, also work.

Use it from your own Telegram bot

Send card photos to your own bot and get the contact file back in the chat.

  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and follow the steps.
  2. Copy the token it gives you and paste it here.

Your token is stored encrypted and only used to reply in your own chat. Connecting replaces any webhook the bot already had.

Connected to .

Last step: open your bot with this button and press Start. That chat becomes the only one the bot answers.

Open bot in Telegram

Photos go to the card reader and are not kept. Contacts are saved directly from your browser to your own Google account; this site never sees them. Privacy.
