Add it to your home screen
  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

What it checks. The .com, .my and .com.my domains, plus Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Telegram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn — for the brand and each branch you switch on. Type a branch of your own in "+ your own" and press Enter; up to 8 at once.

Free / Taken / Check. Free means no one has it. Taken means it's registered. Check means the site hides it behind a login or blocked the lookup — tap the tile to look yourself.

The meter and the glow. Each Free counts fully and each Check counts a third. The more of a name is still open, the brighter the sign and the higher the needle.

Look yourself. "Look at the grey ones" opens each Check site in one other tab, where you're already logged in. Tap Free or Taken for what you see and it moves on to the next; your answers count in the meter.

Underscores. When berjayafood is taken, it also tries berjaya_food and shows it under the tile if that one is free.
