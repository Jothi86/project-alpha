Every morning, go through my Gmail inbox and prepare reply drafts. Never send any email.

1. Look at the emails that arrived in my inbox in the last 24 hours.
2. Skip newsletters, circulars, adverts and automatic notifications (anything with an unsubscribe link, or sent from a no-reply address).
3. For each remaining email that needs an answer from me, create a Gmail DRAFT reply in the same thread:
   - Write in the same language the sender used. Malay gets a Malay reply, English gets English.
   - Keep it short, polite and {{TONE}}.
   - If you don't have a fact you need, write [CHECK: what is missing] in the draft instead of guessing.
   - Sign off as: {{NAME}}
4. Do not send, delete, archive or label anything. Only create drafts.

When you finish, write a short summary: how many emails came in, how many you skipped, and one line per draft (who it is to and what it says).
